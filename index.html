How this library works

Books are downloaded from Project Gutenberg only after you choose import. Text is split into passages and saved in IndexedDB, then retrieved locally as evidence for Expert 802 AI. No book text is uploaded to the Expert 802 server. Large books may be skipped if they exceed the safe per-book size limit; scanned-image books need OCR and are not imported as searchable text.

How knowledge is used

Dictionary and corpus passages are retrieved as evidence. The cognitive core combines this evidence with intent frames, dialogue state and deterministic reasoning. It does not call an external model provider.
